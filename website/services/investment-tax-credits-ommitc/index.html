Investment Tax Credits (OMMITC)

Identify qualifying investments, structure claims strategically and integrate OMMITC within a broader funding strategy.

Book an OMMITC Review → See Our Approach

The Ontario Made Manufacturing Investment Tax Credit rewards manufacturers and processors that invest in Ontario buildings, machinery and equipment. For property that becomes available for use from May 15, 2025, the rate is 15% on up to $20 million of eligible spending a year, a credit of up to $3 million annually.

IIG identifies which of your capital investments qualify, times and structures the claim, and integrates OMMITC with SR&ED, grants and financing so each program adds to the others rather than reducing them.

Eligibility

Who Can Claim

The credit is available to corporations that:

  • Manufacture or process goods in Ontario through a permanent establishment such as a plant or office
  • Are Canadian-controlled private corporations, which receive a refundable credit
  • Are other corporations, including public and foreign-controlled companies, which receive a non-refundable credit with a 10-year carryforward
  • Invest in qualifying manufacturing or processing buildings (Class 1)
  • Invest in qualifying machinery and equipment used in manufacturing or processing (Class 53, and Class 43 after 2025)
The Credit

How the Credit Works

The credit is claimed on your corporate tax return, administered by the CRA on Ontario's behalf.

15%Credit rate on eligible expenditures for property available for use from May 15, 2025 to December 31, 2029
$3 millionMaximum credit per year, on up to $20 million of eligible expenditures shared across an associated group
RefundableFor CCPCs. Other corporations receive a non-refundable credit that can be carried forward 10 years
Five yearsProperty sold, converted or moved out of Ontario within five years may require a repayment

Based on the Government of Ontario's published rules. The credit is scheduled to be repealed effective January 1, 2030. IIG confirms eligibility for each asset before you claim. This page is general information, not tax advice.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question not covered here? Ask our team

Is the credit refundable?

For Canadian-controlled private corporations, yes. Other corporations receive a non-refundable credit through the Expanded OMMITC, which can be carried forward up to 10 years.

Do buildings qualify, or only equipment?

Both. Manufacturing or processing buildings in Ontario that qualify for the additional capital cost allowance are eligible, as well as qualifying machinery and equipment.

When does an investment count?

The credit is based on when property becomes available for use under federal tax rules, generally when you can begin claiming capital cost allowance on it.

We received a grant for the same equipment. Can we still claim?

Often yes, but other government assistance can reduce the eligible amount. We model the combination so you keep as much of both as possible.

Is the limit shared across our group?

Yes. Associated corporations share the $20 million annual expenditure limit.
